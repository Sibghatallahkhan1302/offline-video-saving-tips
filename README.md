# Offline Video Saving Tips

Watching without a connection is easier when your videos are stored as MP4 files. A [youtube to mp4](https://yt2mp4.live/) converter helps you prepare clips before a flight, a commute or a trip with weak signal. These tips keep the process smooth.

## Plan your storage

A minute of 1080p video can take tens of megabytes, so check free space before you save a long playlist.

## Choose quality wisely

Pick 720p for small phone screens and 1080p for tablets, laptops and TVs. Lower resolutions save space and battery.

## Keep files organized

Use clear file names and one folder per topic or trip, so you can find a video quickly when you are offline.

## Stay on the right side of copyright

Save only content you own, have permission to use, or that is openly licensed.
